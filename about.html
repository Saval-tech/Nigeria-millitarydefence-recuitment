About the Nigerian Military

Learn about Nigeria's Armed Forces, their roles, recruitment opportunities and the general requirements for joining military service.

ABOUT THE ARMED FORCES

Serving and Defending Nigeria

The Armed Forces of Nigeria are responsible for protecting the sovereignty, territorial integrity and national security of Nigeria.

The Nigerian Armed Forces consist of three principal services: the Nigerian Army, the Nigerian Navy and the Nigerian Air Force. Each service has its own responsibilities while working together to support national defence and security.

Military service requires discipline, commitment, physical fitness, integrity and a willingness to serve the nation.

THE THREE SERVICES

Nigeria's Armed Forces

The Nigerian military is made up of three major services, each with different responsibilities.

Nigerian Army

The Nigerian Army is the land component of the Armed Forces of Nigeria. It is responsible for land-based military operations and supports national defence and security operations.

Nigerian Navy

The Nigerian Navy is responsible for maritime defence and security. Its duties include protecting Nigeria's territorial waters and supporting maritime operations.

Nigerian Air Force

The Nigerian Air Force provides air power in support of national defence and security. Its responsibilities include protecting Nigeria's airspace and conducting air operations.

JOIN THE MILITARY

Recruitment Requirements

Recruitment requirements depend on the service, recruitment category and particular recruitment exercise. Applicants should always check the official announcement for the exercise they intend to apply for.

Nigerian Citizenship

Applicants generally need to meet the nationality requirements stated in the particular recruitment exercise. For example, current Nigerian Army SSCC requirements state that applicants must be Nigerian citizens by birth.

Age Requirement

Applicants must fall within the age range specified for the recruitment category. Different entry routes can have different age limits.

Educational Qualification

The required educational qualification depends on the entry category. Recruitment exercises may accept qualifications such as secondary school certificates, NCE, OND, HND or university degrees for specific categories.

Height and Physical Standards

Some recruitment categories specify minimum height requirements. Applicants must also meet the physical standards stated in the official recruitment guidelines.

Medical and Physical Fitness

Candidates are expected to meet the medical, physical and psychological fitness standards specified by the service and recruitment category.

Required Documents

Applicants may be required to provide documents such as birth certificate or age declaration, educational certificates, certificate of state of origin, NIN and other documents listed in the specific recruitment advertisement.

Good Character

Applicants must satisfy the character and conduct requirements stated in the applicable recruitment exercise. Criminal convictions can make an applicant ineligible.

Commitment and Discipline

Military service requires discipline, dedication, teamwork, physical readiness and commitment to serving Nigeria.

How to Join

04

05

Begin Training

Successful candidates proceed to the training programme specified by their service and entry category.

Important Recruitment Notice

Recruitment into the Nigerian Armed Forces is conducted through official recruitment channels. Do not pay anyone who claims they can guarantee recruitment or selection.

Requirements, age limits, qualifications, dates and other conditions can change between recruitment exercises. Always read the official recruitment advertisement before applying.

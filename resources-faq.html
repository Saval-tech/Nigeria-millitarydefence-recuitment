What are the basic eligibility requirements for Nigerian military recruitment?

1.Nationality: Must be a Nigerian citizen by birth.
2. Age Limits: Typically between 18 and 22 years old for non-tradesmen/women, and up to 26 or 35 years old for tradesmen/women, medical personnel, and certain specialized roles depending on the service branch (Army, Navy, or Air Force).
3.Identification: Must possess a valid National Identification Number (NIN) and state/local government attestation form.

What are the minimum educational qualifications required?

Must possess at least 4 to 5 credits (including English Language and Mathematics) in GCE, SSCE (WAEC, NECO, NABTEB) in not more than two sittings.

Is the application form or registration fee required?

No. Application and registration on the official recruitment portals are completely free of charge. The military does not request payment or purchase codes through third-party agents or personal bank accounts.

What happens after the online application phase?

Shortlisted candidates are invited to attend a State Screening Exercise at designated centers within their respective states of origin.

What documents must I bring to the screening center?

1. Printed copy of the online application form and guarantor form.
2.Valid National Identification Number (NIN) or slip.
3. Original and photocopies of educational certificates (SSCE/ND, etc.).
4. Birth certificate or age declaration.
5. State of origin and local government attestation certificates.
6. Recent passport photographs.
